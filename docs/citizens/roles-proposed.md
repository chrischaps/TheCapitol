# Citizens' callings: proposed

*A report, 2026-10-25, for the callings plan (`the-capitol-callings.md`, #526; work packages #527 to #543). It describes what citizens will be once the plan is built. [roles-today](roles-today.md) is what they are now. Where this report and the plan differ, the plan is right.*

## The idea in one paragraph

A citizen stops being a job and becomes a person with a **calling**: an activity it would rather be doing, and how strongly it keeps to it. It works in **stints**, a while at one thing and then another, chosen the way a person chooses their evening. The choice weighs its calling, what it already owns, what pays today and what it's done lately. It'll take any commission or wage it's willing to work, sell what it has spare, buy rather than climb the tool tree, trade once it's rich enough, and hire when it has a bench and money. Now and then it does something unrelated. Some citizens have no favourite at all. Later, leanings drift, as a person finds their niche.

## Who a citizen is

| Part | What it is | Drawn from |
|---|---|---|
| Birth trade | one of the eight jobs. It sets the surname and home zone as today, and is the favourite unless the citizen is an all-rounder | the roster's `role` |
| Style | specialist, leaner or all-rounder | `[callings] mix` (40 / 40 / 20) |
| Favourite | the job it comes back to; none for an all-rounder | the birth trade |
| Focus | its favourite's target share of work time | the style, with some spread |
| Leanings | target shares for the other seven jobs; everyone leans at least a little toward everything it can reach | the persona's stream, drawn last |
| Wander | how often it takes a detour in work hours | the persona's stream |
| Temperament | thrift, risk, sociability, patience, as today. Patience sets how long its stints run; thrift how readily it buys rather than makes | unchanged |

### The three styles

| Style | Share of citizens | Favourite's share of work time | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Specialist** | 40% | about 70% | "I'm a smith." Works its trade most of the day, and also gathers its own inputs when the book is thin, takes a well-paid commission in a neighbouring trade, and sells what it has spare. |
| **Leaner** | 40% | about 45% | "I mostly mine, but I'll do anything." Half its time on the favourite and the rest spread widely. The middle of the town. |
| **All-rounder** | 20% | none (no job over about 25%) | "A bit of everything." Dabbles across all eight. The town's handyman, and the first to take an odd commission. |

### Three sample citizens

- **Ada Reeder, leaner.** Favourite: fiber (about 45%).
  - She pulls grass in the rural ring most mornings and banks it at the exchange.
  - Most days she also takes a stint at the sawbench she bought with that money, and sells the planks at her stall.
  - Rich enough by the second game day, she spends an hour making a market in rope and planks.
  - She walks to a wild lake most days she's on.
- **Percy Cutler, specialist.** Favourite: tools (about 70%).
  - Works the anvil most of the day, and sells spare picks and sickles at the hall.
  - When the ingot book is empty, he spends a stint smelting his own ingots.
  - He hires a gatherer for stone when his chest runs low.
  - Now and then he tries a recipe he's never made.
- **Nell Hewitt, all-rounder.** No favourite, though her name is a woodcutter's: the birth trade still names her.
  - One evening: an hour felling trees, a commission for twenty bricks she can just about fire, and a turn round the bazaar lane.
  - On her next session: an ore stint in the foothills, then a wage at someone's forge.

## The activities

A calling leans over eight jobs:

| Activity | Spirit | What it involves | What it brings in from others |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fiber** | the grass and the rope; where money comes from | pulling grass, twisting rope, banking at the exchange | sickles (K2.1), and nothing else. That's why a fiber-lean also does other things |
| **Wood** | the woodcutter | felling trees and old growth, gathering branches | axes |
| **Ore** | the miner | iron from veins, out to the wilds when it pays | picks; commissions pull it to the ranges |
| **Planks** | the sawyer | sawing logs at a sawbench | logs; a sawbench kit |
| **Ingots** | the smelter | smelting ore with charcoal at a forge and kiln | ore, charcoal; kits |
| **Tools** | the toolmaker | stone and iron tools at a workbench and anvil; **sells them** (K2.1) | stone, planks, ingots |
| **Building** | the homebuilder | stations on its lot, bricks; floors once #478 lands | stone, logs, ingots, planks, charcoal |
| **Trading** | the market maker and stall keeper | a market in a few goods at the hall, or a stall of bought goods, once it has its float (about 600 Strands) | its float |

Stone, clay and charcoal are gathered on the way to these, not as leanings.

### Pulls: the Commons in anyone's day

These interrupt a stint, whatever the calling:
- **A commission** it can meet in any trade it's willing to work. A specialist's willing trades are fewer, an all-rounder's are all of them.
- **A wage** at someone's station or lot, in a trade it's willing to work.
- **A bid** well over the book for something it has.
- **Hiring** (K2.4). With an idle station or a lot with nodes, and Strands to spare, it offers piece-rate work.
- **Patronage** (K2.3). With Strands well past its float, it posts commissions for what its home lacks.

### Detours: the unrelated things

Each is about a quarter hour, in work hours, as often as its `wander` says:
- **Try something new:** craft a recipe it has never made, with what it holds.
- **See a place:** a wild lake, a ford, a ridge top or a park it hasn't been to.
- **Browse:** walk the bazaar lane, or look at a stall.
- **Read:** the Gazette or the Wanted page at the board.
- **Call on someone:** a neighbour's lot, or someone it's traded with.

Free time is as today: rest, the hall's steps, the lane, a park, a bridge at dusk.

## What becomes of each role

| Role today | Becomes | What changes for it |
|---|---|---|
| Fiber gatherer | a calling with fiber as favourite (any style) | It still banks fiber, the faucet, on fiber stints. Between them it saws, smelts, builds or trades, so its Strands buy logs, ore and kits. Its wants are the #403 fix. |
| Wood gatherer | wood as favourite | Also buys an axe instead of making one, saws its own logs on a dabble, and takes any commission it's willing to work. |
| Ore gatherer | ore as favourite | Still goes to the wilds for well-paid ore. So does anyone else on an ore stint. |
| Plank crafter | planks as favourite | Gathers its own logs when the book is thin; sells whatever it has spare, not only planks. |
| Ingot crafter | ingots as favourite | As the plank crafter, with ore and charcoal. |
| Tool crafter | tools as favourite | **Sells tools** (K2.1). Its trade finally reaches anyone. |
| Builder | building as favourite | Its lot is still sized for its calling. It posts commissions for its home once it's rich (K2.3). |
| Merchant | trading as favourite | Not a separate kind of citizen any more. Anyone with a trading lean and a float makes a market; the merchant is the one who'd rather be doing it. `--role merchant` mints that favourite. |
| (none) | **all-rounder** | New. No favourite, dabbles in everything; about a fifth of the town. |

## Before and after

What a citizen whose calling is X may do. *Today* means a citizen of role X. *Proposed* means a citizen with X as its favourite.

| May it… | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| work at another trade? | only as a prerequisite | yes: stints at any job it leans to, more of its favourite |
| sell? | only its product (fiber and tool crafters: nothing) | its favourite's product and anything spare from other stints |
| buy and bid? | only its role's inputs (gatherers: nothing) | the inputs of the job it's on and the ones it's heading for; tools before climbing |
| claim commissions? | only for its product | for anything it's willing to make |
| take wages? | only at its trade's stations | in any trade it's willing to work |
| post commissions? | crafters and builders, for inputs | the same, and anyone rich for its home (K2.3) |
| hire? | never | yes, with an idle station and money (K2.4) |
| make a market? | merchants only | anyone with a trading lean and its float (K2.2) |
| keep a stall? | of its product | of its own goods or ones it bought |
| go to the wilds? | by risk; ore gatherers for commissions | by risk; anyone on an ore stint for commissions |
| detour in work hours? | no | yes, by `wander` |
| join a guild? | one naming its trade | one naming any trade it leans to, its favourite first |
| know what others do? | from their surname | from their card's "Lately busy with" (K3.1) |
| change over time? | never | later: its leanings drift toward what paid (K5.1) |
